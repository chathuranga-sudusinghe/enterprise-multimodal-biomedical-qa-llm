# Dataset Plan

Dataset use must be governed by licensing, privacy, de-identification, and enterprise compliance requirements.

## Planned Dataset Categories

- Public clinical summarization datasets.
- De-identified clinical note datasets.
- Medical question-answering datasets used for support and evaluation.
- Multimodal healthcare datasets where approved for research or enterprise use.
- Internal datasets only after formal data governance review.

## Data Rules

- Do not commit real patient data.
- Do not commit secrets or access tokens.
- Prefer small synthetic examples for tests and documentation.
- Validate de-identification and licensing before any training work.
