# Safety Governance

Healthcare LLM development requires explicit safety boundaries, review gates, and auditability.

## Safety Boundary

This system is a clinical documentation and clinician-support assistant. It is not an autonomous diagnosis or treatment system. Final clinical decisions must be made by qualified healthcare professionals.

## Governance Principles

- Keep clinicians in the loop.
- Avoid unsupported diagnosis or treatment claims.
- Track dataset provenance and licensing.
- Require de-identification review for clinical data.
- Maintain evaluation records for safety-sensitive behavior.
- Review deployment readiness before production use.
