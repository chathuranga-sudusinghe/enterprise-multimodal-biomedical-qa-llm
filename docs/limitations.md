# Limitations

## Model and Experiment Limitations

- No foundation model has been loaded or trained.
- No prompt-only baseline inference has been executed.
- No SFT, LoRA, QLoRA, DPO, safety-tuning, or multimodal training run exists.
- No adapters, checkpoints, metrics, comparison reports, or experiment logs exist.
- Qwen2.5-Omni is a proposed foundation-model direction, not an integrated dependency.

## Data and Modality Limitations

- The unified QA helpers prepare text and image-path records; image-path schema preparation does not equal multimodal training or visual understanding.
- No processed dataset has been generated by a tracked end-to-end pipeline.
- Audio and video are future scope only.
- Local raw datasets and generated inspection reports are excluded from Git.
- Dataset source, version, license, access date, and checksum records still require human confirmation.

## Safety and Clinical Limitations

- No clinical validation, medical-device assessment, or patient-outcome study exists.
- Safety boundaries are documented, but safety is not enforced by a trained or evaluated model.
- The project must not be used for diagnosis, treatment, prescription, patient-specific advice, or autonomous clinical decisions.
- Dataset answers are supervision candidates, not verified clinical recommendations.

## Reproducibility Limitations

Current reproducibility applies to the tracked inspection and schema utilities when compatible local inputs are supplied. It does not apply to model experiments because no model revision, hardware profile, seed, training configuration, run ID, adapter metadata, or metrics have been produced.
